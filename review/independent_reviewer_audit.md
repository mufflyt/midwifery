# Independent review of the federal database accuracy manuscript

**Decision on the November 24 review draft: not ready for submission.** This decision applies to `federal_database_accuracy_revised_20261124_202326.docx`, the QMD with its subsequent ZIP code edits, and the regenerated cohort image. Several headline interpretations fail against individual-level inputs even though the two new test programs pass. A later corrected draft requires a separate verification record.

## Findings, ranked by severity

1. **P0 — The 63.1% endpoint does not measure discordance in 2026.** The historical OIG summary produces 563 by subtracting 145 and 184 directional adoptions from 892 initially discordant NPIs. The two events track whether either source ever took the other's 2021 state. Both events can occur for one NPI, and a pair can converge on a third state or leave observation. The producer never restricts this calculation to pairs observed in 2026. Its sidecar also pins a different version of `midwife_panel.csv` from the current local file. I reproduced 563/892 as historical arithmetic only. A direct NPI endpoint join on the current files, following the producer's state cleaning and first-state rule, yields the following counts. These are reviewer diagnostics pending a versioned analysis. The baseline count, comparable follow-up count and missingness each need explicit reporting.

   | Current analysis population | Discordant in 2021 | Both observed in 2026 | Discordant in 2026 | Concordant in 2026 | Unobserved in at least one source |
   |---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
   | Unrestricted source overlap | 939 | 722 | 399 | 323 | 217 |
   | Within the 16,775 frozen NPIs | 913 | 701 | 389 | 312 | 212 |
   | Within the 12,170 active primary-tier NPIs | 784 | 644 | 343 | 301 | 140 |

   In unrestricted follow-up, 18 NPIs satisfied both directional adoption criteria. “Never resolved” also exceeds a finite endpoint. Use a directly observed endpoint, specify whether intervening agreement is allowed, name the target population and disclose selection from loss to follow-up. A Wilson interval on 563/892 cannot repair an incorrect numerator definition. Evidence: `remake_oig_07_09_00440_midwifery.R`, lines 1128–1175; `mysteryroster::attribute_lead_lag_duration`; OIG update-lag CSV and sidecar; frozen crosswalk, NPPES panel and PECOS revalidation panel.

2. **P0 — Medicaid enrollment, comparison eligibility and simultaneity are conflated.** The classification CSV equals `medicaid_enrollment_concordance_any_time_by_year.csv`. The state set contains states touched at any point in a calendar year. Sequential enrollments can qualify. The denominator 53,414 is 85,190 less 31,776 classified “Not observed” and includes 18,898 “Indeterminate.” The source's denominator artifact reports 35,569 provider-years with any qualifying enrollment and 34,516 with comparable evidence from both sources. The historical 16.1% equals (7,050 + 1,531)/53,414 and measures the share assigned one of two multistate classes under this composite denominator. The 27,175 concordant classifications equal 20,125 + 7,050. The 85,190 frame is 12,170 active primary-tier clinicians × seven years, 2018–2024. The separate 107,386 comparable NPI-years span **2013–2024**. Their 2018–2024 subtotal is 75,367. Both year range and inclusion rule distinguish the universes. A separate snapshot-date artifact actually measures simultaneous enrollment on an observed date, with 7,596 multistate classifications among 32,952 comparable pairs. Explicitly distinguish any-time, same-date, ever-enrolled and practice. Supplemental Figure S4 counts persons ever enrolled across states but its graphic labels this “Multi-state practice.” Evidence: `analyze_medicaid_enrollment_concordance.R`, `R/lib/medicaid_state_concordance.R`, both concordance-by-year artifacts, `medicaid_enrollment_concordance_denominators.csv` and `medicaid_set_membership_by_year.csv`.

3. **P0 — The published licensure measure is a state-board verification yield in selected listings.** The OIG producer assigns each record's outcome from board match and board license status. It does not compare a federal license-status field with a state-board status field. Its NPPES group is restricted to the active primary linkage tier. The PECOS group is formed by intersecting the same board outcomes with PECOS enrollment. Describing PECOS as performing comparably on license-status recording, or presenting 94.05% and 92.58% as accuracy of status recorded by NPPES, is unsupported. The fractions are correct: 1,153/1,226 and 1,796/1,940. Across seven states, 1,253 of 3,193 are unresolved, and extreme assumptions span 56.2%–95.5%. Report what the external board confirmed and describe each subgroup. Keep normalized first/last-name matching against the certification roster separate. Methods still says accuracy was assessed for both attributes. Evidence: `remake_oig_07_09_00440_midwifery.R`, lines 1201–1251 and 1640–1661; `oig_table2_inaccuracy_subtypes_by_variable.csv`.

4. **P0 — The dependence-aware trend is outside the stated cohort and describes a different series.** I independently reproduced the CR1 slope, −0.16975 percentage points/year (95% CI −0.25823 to −0.08127), from 44,806 NPI-years and 9,514 NPIs. Of these, **991 NPIs do not belong to the frozen 16,775**. The estimator gives one vote to each NPI-year whose state sets intersect. Published 2013 and 2025 record-weighted agreements are 95.1% and 92.6%, compared with 95.526% and 93.729% for the new NPI-year series. Supplemental Figure S3 still plots the record-weighted series and its WLS line. Its caption points readers to a model for a different series. The older WLS fit also stops in 2025, although Table 4 continues into 2026. Restrict inference to a documented cohort, reconcile the rows and graphics, and retain the original trend as descriptive or unresolved if it cannot be recovered under the prespecified analysis. The negative coefficient must be stated as a signed slope or as a decline of positive magnitude. Pooled S6 results for the four other sources passed a separate frozen-cohort membership check. Evidence: `build_dac_agreement_cluster_trend.R`, its trend CSV, `amcb_npi_linkage_FROZEN.csv`, `state_source_audit_agreement.csv` and `make_state_source_audit_figures.R`.

5. **P0 — Principal comparisons and the denominator ledger still disagree.** The exact any-variable numerator is **4,267/5,877** (`n_any_inconsistent_oig_rule`), compared with the ledger's back-calculated 4,268. Both round to 72.6%. The 2026 ZIP code and state fractions are 2,725/5,877 and 546/5,876. These are field-comparable provider counts with an any-shared-value test. Methods and Table 3 need that overlap rule and the active-primary population. Table 4 reports a separate source-record-weighted analysis, including 21,021/21,759 comparable Open Payments records from 4,311 NPIs in 2025. A distinction between these units in the column labels is welcome but does not establish cohort inclusion or interchangeability of estimates. The ledger omits most of the six Table 2, seven Table 3 and 52 Table 4 rows, along with the abstract's 135/144 formerly-correct fraction. The matrix assigns evidence to a number of conclusions but omits or miscites others, including the claims lead/lag finding and the purported 1.2% update-lag finding. Table 1 has 11 administrative systems plus the AMCB frame; its compressed Facility Affiliation span hides 2023–2025 missing years. The full 2007–2026 registry series contradicts “no single source covers” that period. The abstract calls all comparisons same-year despite current-only comparisons. Evidence: OIG Tables 2 and 4 CSVs, `state_source_audit_agreement.csv`, `state_source_audit_coverage.csv`, all three review ledgers and DOCX Tables 1–4.

6. **P0 — The tests pass several of the precise defects the plan calls for them to catch.** Both published programs passed in this review (21 checks and 12 planted faults). In independent temporary copies, all four of the following returned success: increasing the excluded no-candidate branch by one, replacing only the abstract's ZIP code percentage with 12.3%, inserting an assertion of geographic ground truth, and populating the “cluster-bootstrap” interval column with Wilson limits. The positive gate mainly checks ledger arithmetic and word presence. It does not read the rendered manuscript, claim matrix or most authoritative source values. Expand the gate to check every branch independently against frozen dispositions and every protected fraction, unit, time window and interval against the actual displayed value. Methods promises Wilson intervals for proportions broadly, while Table 4, the any-variable headline, the record-age strata and several other results give none. Specify the estimands receiving Wilson intervals or provide appropriate bounds; repeated records from one clinician need an explicitly justified dependence assumption. Table S6 labels a ratio of CI widths “Design effect”; the conventional design effect is a variance ratio. The four pooled bootstrap rows do resample clinicians, and the annual bootstrap cells are blank as claimed. Evidence: both remediation test files, `build_f2_f3_uncertainty_denominators.R`, `agreement_cluster_bootstrap_ci.csv`, and Tables 2–4/S6.

7. **P1 — Some supplementary and Discussion interpretations exceed the measured outcomes.** In S3 the residual labeled “Left observation” comes from `attribute_lead_lag_matrix`, which requires paired, nonmissing observations in both sources in both years before counting a transition. The residual represents other observed patterns, which include swaps or third-state changes. Its “Still discordant” category specifically requires both baseline values to persist. Registry visibility in S4 cannot distinguish active practice from workforce exit. “Same variable ordering” and “similar magnitudes” are contradicted by Table 3, where telephone disagreement (70.6%) exceeds ZIP code disagreement (46.4%), while OIG's ZIP code value (88.6%) exceeds telephone disagreement (59.0%). The state-vs-ZIP comparison supports a narrower statement for the tested pair. The broadly worded “flat” trends, city-level conclusion and source-choice recommendations need their evidentiary scope stated. Geography has no independent truth source in this study.

8. **P1/P2 — Cohort arithmetic passes, but presentation and final delivery need follow-through.** I counted 14,956 primary and 1,819 nursing-tier distinct certification numbers and NPIs in the freeze. The two tiers comprise 16,775 unique NPIs. The five excluded branches total 5,582 (2,613 + 2,689 + 109 + 8 + 163), reconciling to 22,357. No separate final-disposition count for manual adjudication is reported. The current new Figure 1 improves label fit but exclusion arrows cross the included-cohort node. Its older version is embedded in the inspected draft. Outcome-specific membership filters and the unavailable nursing-tier exclusion analysis must appear in the findings ledger. The title “Certified Midwife Workforce” can denote the CM credential alone and the AMCB Table 1 row omits CMs. A consistent body-paragraph comparison from précis through conclusion, omitting headings, displays/captions and references, counted 3,369 words in the original Downloads Word file and 3,606 in the reviewed draft (+7.0%). The 10–15% reduction target was missed on that definition. The Word package contains 11 tables (five main and six supplemental), nine images, numbered captions, and no raw citation tokens, standalone NA or pending tokens in visible text. All nine embedded images and the regenerated flow image were inspected. Final page-layout verification and placement of a matching timestamped copy in Downloads are pending. The ledger under review still lists every finding as partial, including steps already performed.

## Verification and release conditions

I compared all declared input and code file SHA-256 digests for the new cohort and trend CSVs and regenerated bootstrap CSV against their sidecars; every listed digest matched. This verifies those file versions, not their scientific definitions. The old OIG endpoint sidecar's NPPES panel digest is `40c45ce1…`; the current panel digest is `6b9696ce…`. The supplied claim matrix, denominator ledger, findings ledger, methods note and change log were reviewed alongside the implementation plan, statistical and figure producers, source artifacts, test code, QMD and Word XML. I did not modify the analysis or manuscript.

Release requires a cohort-bound endpoint with attrition, a defensible licensure construct, correctly labeled Medicaid denominators and timing, a reconciled or openly unresolved historical trend, source-bound numerical checks, and a rerendered Word file inspected against the corrected results. Record any unavailable analyses and exact residual uncertainty in the final ledger. This report describes the review snapshot; a later response to each finding should identify the corrected file or artifact and verification evidence.
