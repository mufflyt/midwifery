Methods and limitations

Scope and limitations

The cohort is AMCB-certified CNMs and CMs only. Certified professional midwives and state-licensed direct-entry midwives are outside it, so midwifery access is understated, unevenly by state.

Geographic persistence, 2007–2025

How often a midwife stays in the same county: 95.9% year to year, 67.5% across a career.

Sources. All public: the AMCB certification directory, the CMS National Plan and Provider Enumeration System, US Census geography, and USDA Rural-Urban Continuum Codes.

What these maps show. Last-observed practice location, not confirmed current practice. They are a distribution of practice locations rather than a measure of access: patients cross county lines, and not every certificant is linked to a location, so a county with no marker is not a county without midwifery care.
